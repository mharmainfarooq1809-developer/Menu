Our story

Cooked with care, served with warmth.

The Food House began with one simple idea: a neighbourhood kitchen should feel like someone's home. Our burgers are ground daily, our pizza dough is fermented slowly, and everything that leaves the charcoal grill is made to order.

We source fresh produce from local suppliers and keep our menu short so every plate gets the attention it deserves.

  • Fresh, locally sourced ingredients
  • Prepared to order — never held under a lamp
  • Charcoal grill and stone-baked pizza
